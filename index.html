Behind Crafting the Mind

“I created Crafting the Mind because I believe becoming better shouldn't require people to lose themselves in the process. I wanted to create a space for people who are figuring things out, people navigating purpose, clarity, work, relationships, growth and the everyday work of being human.”

“CTM started with conversations about how we think, work, grow and navigate life, with mindset at the centre. Today, it has grown into a community where people can learn, build, discover opportunities and become better together.”
